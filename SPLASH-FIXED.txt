Syslinux Requirements:
• Resolution: 640x480 (standard VGA)
• Format: PNG, RGB or indexed color
• No transparency (RGBA causes issues)
• File size: Keep under 1MB

GRUB Requirements:
• Any resolution (auto-scales)
• PNG format
• Can handle transparency

Your Original Image:
• Size: 923x234 pixels
• Format: RGBA (with transparency)
• Problem: Doesn't fit standard VGA resolution

New Syslinux Image:
• Size: 640x480 pixels

• Result: Will display properly!
