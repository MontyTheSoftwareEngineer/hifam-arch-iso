🎨 Customization Tips:
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Want to change the logo?
  1. Replace the PNG files:
     • ~/hifam-arch/syslinux/splash.png
     • ~/hifam-arch/grub/splash.png
     • ~/hifam-arch/airootfs/usr/share/plymouth/themes/hifam/logo.png

  2. Rebuild ISO: ./build-iso.sh

Recommended logo specs:
  • Format: PNG with transparency support
  • Syslinux: 640x480 or 800x600 recommended
  • GRUB: Any size (auto-scaled)
  • Plymouth: 512x512 or larger for best quality

Want different animations?
  • Edit: ~/hifam-arch/airootfs/usr/share/plymouth/themes/hifam/hifam.script
  • Plymouth script language documentation:
    https://www.freedesktop.org/wiki/Software/Plymouth/Scripts/
